Rejection Candle Entry

1
Take the first externalIn NY, wait for price to take the first external — while keeping your protected zone in mind the whole time.
2
Fake reaction + inducementWait for the fake reaction (FR) and the inducement to set the trap before anything else.
3
Big rejection candleIf you see a big rejection candle, that is your trigger — take the trade from the rejection candle itself.
4
Target the next externalYour draw on liquidity is the next external. That is where you are taking the trade to.
Invalidation

Invalid if there is no rejection candle — no rejection candle, no entry.

Minor Inducement (Entry from Candle Body)

1
Minor IDM sweptAsia High or External is taken — the local inducement above recent structure.
2

3

Internal Protected

1
Liquidity sweepExternal liquidity is taken — stops below the prior session pool get swept first.
2
Internal BOSPrice prints a break of structure inside the dealing range — the internal shift in intent.
3
Protected zone establishedThe most recent low/high after the BOS becomes the protected zone — never to be closed through.
4
Entry at the ExtremeYou place a pending order under available build-up liquidity, with the protected level as your invalidation.
Invalidation

Invalid the moment price closes back through the protected zone with momentum — your stop sits on the other side of that level, not inside it.

Protected Zone

1
External liquidity sweepStops below the prior session low get swept first.
2
Break of structurePrice prints a clean BOS in the direction of the read.
3
Protected draw on liquidityPullback respects the protected zone — never closes through it.
4
Entry on retraceYou take the trade at the retrace into the BOS origin, not the displacement.
Invalidation

Invalid the moment price closes back through the swept low with momentum, or fails to print a higher-timeframe BOS within the next 4 candles after the sweep.

AMD

1
AccumulationPrice accumulates inside a defined range — the A in AMD.
2
ManipulationA sweep beyond the range engineers liquidity — the M, the trap, the Smart Money Trap (SMT).
3
DistributionPrice distributes in the opposite direction of the manipulation — the D, the real leg.
4
Entry on confirmationTrigger fires on the confirmation candle out of the SMT zone — the cycle confirms.
Invalidation

Invalid if the manipulation leg resolves in the same direction (no SMT — no trap), or if price closes back inside the accumulation range without a confirmation candle.
